01 — Our Story

Why we called it Darna

Darna is an Arabic word for "our home" — the place you don't need an invitation to walk into. That's the room we built on Ball Road: low tables, slow refills, and a menu that moves between a proper flat white and a glass of karak chai without apologizing for either.

"Good coffee gets you in the door. A place to stay is what brings you back."

We roast toward balance rather than intensity, steep tea the way it's steeped at home, and keep the kitchen small on purpose — a short list of Mediterranean bites, made well, instead of a long one made fast.
